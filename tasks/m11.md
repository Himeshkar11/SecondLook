### Why M11 is important

After M10, you have the **feature-complete shape** of SecondLook. M11 is basically the **red-team pass**:

**Bidder A → tries to become Officer → ❌**

**Bidder A → tries to access Bidder B → ❌**

**Bidder A → tries to mark themselves Qualified → ❌**

**Bidder A → tries to access B's private documents → ❌**

**Officer → tries to access unauthorized resources → ❌/according to actual policy**

**Frontend role manipulation → backend still rejects → ❌**

**Client sends `role: "OFFICER"` → backend ignores/rejects → ❌**

That gives you a much stronger foundation before M12, where you'll do the **final end-to-end integration, demo flow, cleanup, documentation, and freeze**.
